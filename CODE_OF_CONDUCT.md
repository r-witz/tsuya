# Code of conduct

tsuya is built by people with different backgrounds, languages and levels of experience. Everyone taking part, in
issues, pull requests, reviews and discussions, is expected to:

- be kind and patient, and assume good intent;
- criticise ideas and code, never people;
- accept that a maintainer may say no, and say no with a reason;
- help newcomers find their way.

Not acceptable: harassment, insults, discriminatory jokes or language, personal attacks, publishing someone's private
information, and any conduct a reasonable person would find unwelcome.

## Reporting

Report a problem privately to the maintainers through the repository's [private reporting
form](https://github.com/r-witz/tsuya/security/advisories/new) (with "Conduct" in the title), or with GitHub's **Report
content** on the comment. Reports are kept confidential.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, lock conversations, and block people who do not follow this code, in
proportion to what happened.
